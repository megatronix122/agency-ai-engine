# 🎯 Agency AI Engine — Master Dashboard

## Quick Start (3 Steps)
1. ⬇️ **Duplicate this template** (top right)
2. 📝 **Fill your first client intake** (see "Intake Forms" below)
3. 🤖 **Run AI prompts** (copy/paste into ChatGPT/Claude)

---

## 📋 Sections

### [1] Client Intake Database
Track every prospect. Fields:
- Company name, website, industry
- Contact person + role
- Budget range ($5k/$10k/$25k/$50k+)
- Services needed (SEO/PPC/Content/Social/Email)
- Discovery call notes
- **Status**: Lead → Qualified → Pitched → Negotiating → Closed → Lost
- **Linked to**: Proposals, Outreach sequences

### [2] Proposal Generator
For each qualified lead:
1. Copy the "Intake Summary"
2. Paste into [AI Prompt #2: Proposal Writer]
3. Get 3-tier proposal (Basic/Pro/Enterprise)
4. Save to "Proposals Sent" database

### [3] Outreach Sequences
Pre-written, AI-personalized sequences:
- Cold email (LinkedIn → Email)
- Follow-up #1 (3 days)
- Follow-up #2 (7 days)
- Breakup email (14 days)
- Re-engagement (90 days)

### [4] Pitch Deck Library
Templates for:
- Initial discovery call deck
- Case study presentations
- Retrospective/re-up decks

### [5] Monthly Reporting
Auto-generate client reports:
- Traffic/lead metrics summary
- What we did this month
- Next month plan
- ROI calculation

### [6] SOPs & Scripts
- Discovery call script (15 min)
- Objection handling guide
- Scope creep email templates
