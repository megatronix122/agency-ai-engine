# Agency AI Engine — Setup Video Script

**Format:** Screen recording + AI voiceover  
**Length:** 8–10 minutes  
**Style:** Clear, concise, no fluff  
**Voice:** Professional but warm, energetic but not hype-y

---

## [0:00–0:30] Hook & Overview

**[Visual: Screen showing Gumroad purchase confirmation page]**

**VOICEOVER:**
"Hey — welcome to the Agency AI Engine. You just made a smart decision. In the next 10 minutes, I'm going to show you exactly how to set up this system so you can start converting strangers into signed clients — faster than you ever thought possible.

Here's what we're covering: First, I'll walk you through the Notion template. Then we'll dive into the 7 AI workflows. And finally, I'll show you how to use this system to close your next deal.

Let's get started."

**[Transition: Zoom into Notion workspace]**

---

## [0:30–2:00] Part 1: Setting Up Your Notion Template

**[Visual: Notion — "Duplicate Template" button click]**

**VOICEOVER:**
"First things first — you need your own copy of the template. Click the 'Duplicate' button in the top right corner of this page. Notion will ask you which workspace to add it to — pick your main agency workspace.

[Pause for visual of duplicating]

Done? Good. Now you have three main sections: the Master Dashboard, your Client Intake Database, and the AI Prompts Library.

Let's start with the dashboard."

**[Visual: Navigate to Master Dashboard]**

**VOICEOVER:**
"This is your command center. You can see your entire pipeline at a glance — how many leads, how many proposals sent, your close rate, and projected revenue.

The beauty of this system is everything connects. A lead in your intake form becomes a proposal, which becomes a project, which becomes a case study for your next pitch.

But first, let's add a real prospect so you can see this in action."

---

## [2:00–4:00] Part 2: Your First Client Intake

**[Visual: Click "New" in Client Intake Database]**

**VOICEOVER:**
"Click 'New' in your Client Intake Database. Here's where the magic starts.

You'll see a form that takes about two minutes to complete. Company name, website, industry, who you're talking to, their budget range, and what services they need.

[Type in example data]

Let's say we're talking to 'Coastal Coffee Roasters' — a boutique coffee brand that needs help with SEO and content marketing. Their budget is 10 to 25 thousand dollars, and we're speaking to their marketing director, Sarah.

The most important field is 'Discovery Call Notes.' This is where you paste your raw notes from the call — what they're struggling with, what they've tried, what they want to achieve.

[Type example notes]

'Sarah mentioned organic traffic flat for 6 months. Tried working with a freelancer before but didn't see results. Wants to rank for local coffee keywords and build email list. Timeline: Q2 launch.'

That's it. Two minutes. Now watch what happens when we use this with the AI prompts."

---

## [4:00–6:00] Part 3: AI Workflow Demo — The Pitch Deck

**[Visual: Navigate to AI Prompts Library, select Prompt #1]**

**VOICEOVER:**
"Open the AI Prompts Library and find Prompt Number One — the Pitch Deck Generator.

This is a copy-paste workflow. You don't need to be an AI expert. You just need to copy this prompt and paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, or whatever AI you prefer.

[Copy prompt text]

Now, see these bracketed sections? [COMPANY_NAME], [INDUSTRY], [PAIN_POINT]? That's where you paste the info from your intake form.

[Replace brackets with example data]

So we replace [COMPANY_NAME] with Coastal Coffee Roasters. [INDUSTRY] becomes Specialty Coffee. And [PAIN_POINT] comes straight from our notes — organic traffic flat for six months, tried freelancers, needs local SEO and email growth.

[Show final prompt]

Copy this complete prompt and paste it into ChatGPT."

**[Visual: Switch to ChatGPT/Claude browser window, paste prompt]**

**VOICEOVER:**
"Hit enter. And in about 30 seconds..."

**[Visual: AI generates response]**

**VOICEOVER:**
"You have a complete five-slide pitch narrative. The hook — what they're missing and what it's costing them. The shift — what's changed in their market. The solution — exactly what you'll do. Proof — a relevant result or benchmark. And the next step — a specific call to action.

This would have taken you an hour to write from scratch. It took two minutes.

Now save this to your Pitch Deck Library in Notion and you're ready for your follow-up call."

---

## [6:00–7:30] Part 4: The Proposal Workflow

**[Visual: Back to Notion, select Prompt #2]**

**VOICEOVER:**
"Let's go deeper. After your pitch call, you need a proposal. This is where agencies lose the most time — and deals.

Prompt Number Two is the Proposal Writer. Same process: copy, paste, fill in your intake data.

But here's what makes this different — it generates a three-tier proposal structure. Foundation, Growth, and Domination.

[Show AI generating proposal]

Why three tiers? Because it changes the conversation. Instead of yes or no, you're asking 'which level of investment makes sense for your goals?'

The AI prices each tier based on your budget range — 30 percent for Foundation, 60 percent for Growth, 100 percent for Domination.

And it includes everything: what each tier includes, timeline, best-fit scenario, even an ROI projection.

[Show completed proposal structure]

Copy this into a Google Doc, add your branding, and send. Total time: ten minutes instead of three hours."

---

## [7:30–8:30] Part 5: Outreach Automation

**[Visual: Navigate to Prompt #3]**

**VOICEOVER:**
"But what about getting leads in the first place? Prompt Three is your Cold Outreach Sequence.

Paste your prospect's info, their company details, and any trigger you found — like new funding, a product launch, or a hiring spree.

The AI generates a four-email sequence. The opener. The value-add follow-up. The social proof nudge. And the breakup email.

Each email is under 125 words. No 'I hope this finds you well.' Just clear, personalized outreach that gets replies.

[Show example emails]

Save these to your Outreach database in Notion and schedule them in your email tool."

---

## [8:30–9:30] Part 6: The Full System

**[Visual: Back to Master Dashboard, show all components]**

**VOICEOVER:**
"So here's your new workflow:

One — capture every lead in the Client Intake Database. Two minutes.

Two — use the AI prompts to generate whatever you need: pitch decks, proposals, outreach, follow-ups, case studies, reports.

Three — track everything in your dashboard so you know your numbers.

The other four prompts work the same way. Follow-up automation for stalled deals. Case study creator to turn wins into future sales. Monthly report generator for client retention. And scope creep defender — because nothing kills margins like unpaid extra work.

Every prompt follows the same copy-paste format. Fill in the brackets. Paste into AI. Get professional output in minutes, not hours."

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Screen Recording Software
- **OBS** (free) — best quality, more setup
- **Loom** (free tier) — easier, cloud storage
- **QuickTime** (Mac) — simple, built-in

### AI Voiceover Options
1. **ElevenLabs** (best quality, paid)
2. **Play.ht** (good quality, free tier)
3. **Murf.ai** (studio quality, paid)
4. **Descript Overdub** (cloned voice option)

### Visual Enhancements (Optional)
- Mouse highlight (Cursor Pro, KeyCastr)
- Zoom in on key UI elements
- Text overlays for key timestamps
- Transition slides between sections

### Music
- Optional: subtle background music
- Volume: 10–15% of voiceover
- Source: Epidemic Sound, Artlist, or Uppbeat
